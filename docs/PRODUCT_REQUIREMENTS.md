# GER NAADGAI — Product Requirements

**ГЭР НААДГАЙ · ᠭᠡᠷ ᠨᠠᠭᠠᠳᠠᠭᠠᠢ**

**Tagline:** MONGOLIA IN YOUR HANDS

> This document is the living product requirements spec for the GER NAADGAI digital ecosystem. It is the source of truth for scope — implementation should be checked against it, and it should be updated whenever requirements change. Sections 1–37 are the founding brief. Sections 38–98 are a later addendum (engagement design, marketplace, wallet, illustration system, Mongolian-first localization, and social sharing).

---

## 1. Core Brand Idea

GER NAADGAI is a modern Mongolian children's collectible brand.

The physical product is a miniature Mongolian ger-shaped package containing:

- one small collectible toy
- one traditional Mongolian-inspired candy
- one collectible/story card
- one unique QR code

The QR code connects the physical collectible to the GER NAADGAI digital world.

The experience is: **OPEN → SCAN → DISCOVER → PLAY → COLLECT → UNLOCK**

The child should feel that every physical collectible is a "key" that unlocks something inside a larger Mongolian world.

This is **not** simply an educational app. It should feel like **70% modern children's collectible/game, 30% Mongolian cultural heritage** — playful, magical, premium, collectible and culturally authentic.

Do **not** make it feel like: a school app, a museum app, a government cultural project, a generic educational game, a generic Asian mobile game, or an overly childish cartoon app.

Think: *"Modern Mongolia made playable and collectible."*

## 2. The 12-Year Collectible System

The central long-term business model is based on Mongolia's 12-year animal zodiac cycle. Every year has a special GER NAADGAI zodiac collection.

The first launch is **2027 — YEAR OF THE SHEEP (ХОНЬ ЖИЛ)**, treated as the first annual limited/special edition.

The 12 animals: Хулгана (Mouse/Rat), Үхэр (Ox/Cow), Бар (Tiger), Туулай (Rabbit), Луу (Dragon), Могой (Snake), Морь (Horse), Хонь (Sheep), Бич (Monkey), Тахиа (Rooster), Нохой (Dog), Гахай (Pig).

The app must be a **reusable 12-year platform**, not hard-coded to 2027. Each zodiac year is data-driven with its own: character, visual identity, story, mini-game, collectible set, badge, digital reward, and limited-edition status. 2027 is simply the first active year.

## 3. First Collection — Five Treasures of Mongolia

**ТАВАН ХОШУУ МАЛ**. Five animals: АДУУ (Horse), ТЭМЭЭ (Camel), ХОНЬ (Sheep), ЯМАА (Goat), ҮХЭР (Cow).

Each has: physical toy, unique character identity, collectible number, QR code, digital character, short cultural story, collectible card, digital badge/progress, mini-game or interaction. The child should want to collect all five.

## 4. Collection Mechanic

Every physical product has a unique QR code. Example: child buys GER NAADGAI Five Treasures #03 (Goat + candy + story card + QR code), scans it, sees "NEW COLLECTIBLE UNLOCKED!", and the Goat becomes part of their digital collection. The user can then: view the character, rotate/view it, read a short story, hear narration, see cultural facts, play a mini interaction/game, collect badges, track collection progress.

## 5. Collection Completion

Core psychological loop: **BUY → OPEN → SCAN → UNLOCK → COLLECT → COMPLETE → REWARD**.

Completing all five Five Treasures triggers a special completion experience ("YOU FOUND ALL FIVE TREASURES!") unlocking: special animation, Five Treasures badge, special story, special mini-game, exclusive digital item, completion certificate/card. It should feel like unlocking a secret world, not a school achievement.

## 6. 2027 Special Edition

ХОНЬ ЖИЛ (Year of the Sheep) is a separate annual zodiac collectible, distinct from the Five Treasures Sheep — its own design, story, color accent, mini-game, badge, and "2027 LIMITED EDITION" marker. In 2027 the app therefore contains the Core Collection (Five Treasures) **plus** the 2027 Special Edition (Year of the Sheep), both collectible by the child.

## 7. Annual Zodiac System

A dedicated **"12 ЖИЛ / THE 12-YEAR JOURNEY"** section shows all 12 animals as collectible slots. Only the current year is fully active; future years appear mysterious and collectible ("Coming Soon"). Tagline options: *"Your 12-year journey begins here."* / *"12 years. 12 animals. One Mongolian story."*

## 8. Long-Term Collector Identity

Every user has a **GER NAADGAI COLLECTOR** profile: avatar, collector name, collection progress, Five Treasures progress, current zodiac collection, badges, unlocked stories, completed mini-games, total collectibles.

## 9. Digital Ger — Home World

The home screen is a beautiful stylized Mongolian ger — the user's personal digital ger/world and main navigation hub (Collection, Stories, Mini Games, 12-Year Journey, Badges, Profile). The ger grows richer as the child collects more items: animals appear around it, story objects/books appear, special ornaments appear on collection completion, zodiac symbols appear on zodiac unlocks.

## 10. Digital Collection Room

A gallery/grid of collectible cards: image, number, Mongolian name, English name, collection name, unlocked/locked state. Locked cards show a silhouette + "???" + "Scan a GER NAADGAI QR code to discover this character" rather than being hidden — the empty slots should make the user want to complete them.

## 11. Character Detail Page

Character illustration, name (Mongolian + English), collection, number, rarity, story ("DISCOVER"), cultural fact ("DID YOU KNOW?"), and a "PLAY WITH [X]" mini-game button.

## 12. Story System

Collections connect to stories (e.g. Эрхий Мэргэн / Erkhii Mergen), unlocked on completion. The child can read, listen to a narrated version, and swipe through illustrated scenes, ending with a "PLAY THE STORY" prompt.

## 13. Mini-Game System

Short (30 sec–3 min), simple, and each tied to the cultural meaning of its character — not random arcade games. Examples: Horse racing/obstacle riding, Camel desert journey/find water, Sheep herding, Goat mountain climbing/collect herbs, Cow pasture activity, Archery (aim and shoot), Shagai/ankle-bone-inspired interaction.

## 14. QR Code Experience

Bridge between physical and digital: scanning screen → scanning animation → "GER NAADGAI FOUND!" → reveal animation → collectible unlock → add to collection → reward. For the prototype, use mock QR codes and demo scan buttons; production should support a unique QR code per physical toy.

## 15. Physical + Digital Business Model

Core commercial loop: **PHYSICAL PRODUCT → QR CODE → DIGITAL CHARACTER → GAME/STORY → COLLECTION → COMPLETION REWARD → DESIRE TO COLLECT MORE PHYSICAL PRODUCTS.** The digital experience must increase desirability of the physical collectible — the app extends the toy's value, it never replaces it.

## 16. Brand Visual Direction

Warm cream background, deep forest green, terracotta/Mongolian red, warm orange, muted sky blue, muted gold, hand-drawn cultural illustrations, modern Mongolian motifs (clouds, ger shapes, mountains, stars, steppe, traditional ornaments). Modern, premium, playful, cultural, warm, collectible, imaginative. Avoid: generic cartoon style, excessive gradients, neon colors, generic Asian aesthetics, cheap toy-store visuals, overly educational interface, excessive traditional ornamentation.

## 17. UI Design

Large rounded cards, soft shadows, generous whitespace, playful but refined typography, illustrated backgrounds, tactile buttons, subtle animation, collectible cards, progress rings, badges, locked/unlocked states. Primary CTAs: DISCOVER / PLAY / COLLECT / UNLOCK. Rounded, friendly typography (Poppins or similar geometric rounded sans-serif). Traditional Mongolian script (ᠭᠡᠷ ᠨᠠᠭᠠᠳᠠᠭᠠᠢ) as secondary decorative/cultural element.

## 18. Main App Structure

**HOME · COLLECTION · PLAY · STORIES · 12 YEARS · PROFILE**

- **Home:** Digital Ger, featured collectible, current year, collection progress.
- **Collection:** All collectibles, Five Treasures, 2027 Sheep, future collections.
- **Play:** Mini-games, unlocked games, coming soon.
- **Stories:** Unlocked stories, locked stories.
- **12 Years:** 12-year zodiac journey.
- **Profile:** Collector identity, badges, progress.

## 19. Home Screen

Emotional centerpiece: a beautiful Mongolian ger at the center, surrounded by collected animals, small cultural objects, clouds, mountains, stars. Top: "GER NAADGAI / YOUR MONGOLIAN WORLD". Main card: current year ("2027 · YEAR OF THE SHEEP / Your journey begins."). Progress: "FIVE TREASURES 3/5 COLLECTED". CTA: "CONTINUE COLLECTING →".

## 20. Collection Screen

Sections: MY COLLECTION → FIVE TREASURES (3/5), 2027 SPECIAL EDITION (1/5), 12-YEAR JOURNEY (1/12), each with a beautiful visual cover.

## 21. 12-Year Journey Screen

A visually beautiful horizontal or circular timeline of the 12 animals, current year ACTIVE, others LOCKED. Should feel like *"This is a 12-year adventure,"* not a zodiac information chart.

## 22. Game Screen

Game selection screen, each game a collectible-style card, with clear lock conditions (e.g. "🐎 HORSE RACE — LOCKED — Collect Horse").

## 23. Story Screen

Illustrated storybook interface: large artwork, minimal text, page progress ("1/8"), swipe to advance, "LISTEN" narration button, and on completion "STORY COMPLETE! UNLOCKED: SPECIAL MINI-GAME".

## 24. Reward System

Badges: FIRST DISCOVERY, FIVE TREASURES, STORY KEEPER, GAME MASTER, YEAR KEEPER, 12-YEAR JOURNEY. Rewards should mostly be badges, digital items, story unlocks, mini-games, ger decorations, character accessories — avoid aggressive monetization.

## 25. Rarity

Levels: COMMON, SPECIAL, RARE, LEGENDARY — introduced carefully. Cultural importance must **not** be determined by rarity. No gambling mechanics; the experience stays child-safe and collection-focused.

## 26. Safety

No: chat with strangers, public messaging, social media posting (uncontrolled), gambling, loot boxes, manipulative dark patterns, unnecessary personal data collection. Safe and parent-friendly by default.

## 27. Parent Value

Parents should immediately understand GER NAADGAI is not just a toy: *"Every collectible opens a new part of Mongolia."* Children discover Mongolian animals, nomadic life, folklore, traditional games, history, nature and culture — naturally, through play, never like homework.

## 28–37. Prototype Requirements, Demo Flow, Technical & Data Architecture

*(Delivered in the v1 prototype — see [`README`](../README.md) / codebase for current implementation status.)*

Build a polished, real-navigation interactive prototype: Home, Collection, Character detail, QR scan simulation, Unlock animation, Five Treasures progress, 2027 Sheep Special Edition, 12-Year Journey, Stories, Mini-games, Profile, Badges — using mock data / local state, no backend required for v1. QR scanning simulated via a "SCAN DEMO QR" button and demo character picker.

Key demo flows to support: Home → Scan → Unlock → Collect (4/5) → Play → Reward → Home ger updates; Collect 5/5 → Five Treasures Complete → Special story unlocked → Special mini-game unlocked; 12-Year Journey → 2027 Sheep Special Edition → Unlocked.

Stack: React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion (or equivalent), Lucide/custom SVG icons, reusable component architecture, centralized mock data (`collections.ts`, `zodiac.ts`, `characters.ts`, `stories.ts`, `games.ts`, `badges.ts`).

Data models are scalable so future developers can add 2028, 2029, etc. without rebuilding the app — see `Character`, `ZodiacYear`, `Collection` shapes in `src/types.ts`.

Design quality bar: must look like a real startup/product prototype presentable to investors, toy manufacturers, parents, retail partners, cultural organizations, and children's product partners — a distinctive GER NAADGAI world, not a generic dashboard or generic children's game UI.

Core UX principle — every screen should answer one of: *What did I discover? What can I collect? What can I play? What can I unlock? What comes next?*

## 38. Attention & Engagement — Child-First Experience

The digital experience must immediately capture a child's attention. **The first 5–10 seconds are critical** — when a child opens the app, the world should feel alive.

Use: subtle character movement, animated clouds, moving grass, stars, small environmental sounds, gentle character reactions, collectible reveal animations, satisfying unlock effects, playful micro-interactions.

Do **not** use overstimulating neon colors or chaotic animations. The experience should be **MAGICAL + TACTILE + CURIOUS + PREMIUM**. The child should constantly feel: *"What is this? What can I discover? What can I unlock? What am I missing? What happens if I complete this collection?"*

## 39. GER NAADGAI Collectible Marketplace

A dedicated **GER NAADGAI MARKET** section where users can: buy new collectibles, browse available collections, see limited editions, see collection progress, exchange eligible duplicate collectibles, and (potentially, future) trade with other users.

**For the first MVP, do not implement unrestricted child-to-child trading.** Instead: **BUY → COLLECT → EXCHANGE DUPLICATES**. It should feel like a premium collectible shop, never a gambling marketplace.

Categories: NEW, FIVE TREASURES, 2027 SPECIAL EDITION, LIMITED EDITION, COLLECTIONS, ACCESSORIES. Each product card: character, collection, collectible number, rarity, price, availability, "ADD TO CART".

## 40. Collectible Exchange System

Users may receive duplicate physical collectibles. Build a future-ready exchange system, e.g. "GOAT #04 · 2 COPIES · [EXCHANGE]" where a duplicate can be exchanged for another eligible collectible, exchange points, or a digital reward. Simulate the exchange flow locally for the MVP; future architecture should allow a secure marketplace/exchange system.

**Important:** because this is a children's product, do not enable direct unrestricted peer-to-peer transactions. All trading/exchange should eventually be **parent-approved, platform-controlled, moderated, and auditable**.

## 41. GER NAADGAI Wallet

A wallet inside the user account, clearly separating two concepts:

- **A. GER NAADGAI COINS** — in-app loyalty/game points. **Not** cryptocurrency, legal tender, withdrawable cash, or transferable money. Used for cosmetic items, selected digital items, game rewards, selected platform features. **No cash-out.**
- **B. Real money / stored value** — see §42.

## 42. Real-Money Wallet Architecture (Future)

Design the architecture to eventually support a **parent-controlled** stored-value wallet, balance shown in Mongolian tögrög (MNT), e.g. "PARENT WALLET — Balance ₮50,000 — Child spending allowance ₮10,000." The child must **not** independently control the real-money wallet. Parent account controls: add money, spending limits, purchase approval, transaction history, refund requests, wallet lock, parental controls. Children see only the amount/allowance necessary for the experience.

**Important: do not implement a real-money wallet or payment processing in the prototype — UI simulation only.** Production architecture must allow a licensed payment provider to be integrated later.

## 43. Internal Game Currency

A separate internal currency (working name **GER COINS** / **NAADGAI POINTS**), **not real money**, e.g. "⭐ 1,250 NAADGAI". Earned by: playing mini-games, completing collections, discovering stories, completing challenges, scanning collectibles. **No** NAADGAI → MNT conversion and **no** cash withdrawal — used primarily for digital rewards.

## 44. Purchase Flow

**MARKET → PRODUCT → ADD TO CART → CHECKOUT → PARENT APPROVAL → PAYMENT → ORDER CONFIRMED.** For the prototype: mock payment only, no real payment processing. Show order summary, shipping, total, payment method, parent approval.

## 45. Parent Account

A separate parent mode / **PARENT CONTROL** dashboard: child profile, collection progress, wallet, purchase history, spending limit, screen-time preferences, privacy settings, notifications. Parent approval is required for real-money purchases. Children should never be encouraged to pressure parents to buy products — avoid manipulative language ("BUY NOW OR YOU WILL MISS OUT!"); use neutral language ("Limited edition available.").

## 46. Collection Economy

The product economy is designed around **DISCOVERY + COLLECTION + PLAY + COMPLETION**, not gambling, randomized money spending, or pay-to-win. Physical products may contain surprise collectibles, but the digital ecosystem must not use paid random loot boxes — real money must never purchase randomized digital rewards.

## 47. Trading / Exchange Safety (Future)

Potential future flow: child requests exchange → parent approval → platform verifies item → exchange completed → transaction recorded. No direct messaging between children. No direct bank transfer between children. No anonymous peer-to-peer trading.

## 48. Attention Loop

**OPEN → DISCOVER → SCAN → UNLOCK → PLAY → EARN → COLLECT → COMPLETE → UNLOCK STORY → UNLOCK MINI-GAME → RETURN TO MARKET → DISCOVER NEXT COLLECTIBLE.** The marketplace is a natural extension of the collection experience — it must not interrupt gameplay constantly.

## 49. Child + Parent Experience

Two users: the **child** (primary user — fun, discovery, collection, games, stories) and the **parent** (economic decision maker — safety, transparency, spending control, educational/cultural value, privacy). Design both experiences separately.

## 50. 12-Year Economic Ecosystem

Each year: new collection → new stories → new mini-games → new physical collectibles → new digital content, then repeat annually. The platform becomes more valuable as the user's collection grows. Long-term concept: *"Collect Mongolia, one year at a time."*

## 51. Marketplace Visual Design

Must **not** look like Alibaba or a generic e-commerce site — it should feel like entering a magical collectible shop inside the GER NAADGAI world: illustrated product cards, large character artwork, collection progress, limited-edition badges, soft animations, the established brand palette (cream, forest green, terracotta, muted blue, gold).

## 52. Wallet Visual Design

Clearly distinguish **NAADGAI POINTS** (digital reward currency, e.g. "⭐ NAADGAI · 1,250 POINTS") from the **PARENT WALLET** (real-money balance, e.g. "₮50,000") — as separate sections. Never visually imply NAADGAI POINTS are equivalent to MNT.

## 53. Production-Ready Architecture

Design the backend/data architecture to eventually support: Users, Parents, Children, Collections, Characters, QR codes, Physical products, Inventory, Orders, Payments, Wallets, Points, Transactions, Stories, Games, Badges, Exchanges, Marketplace listings, Annual zodiac collections. For the MVP: mock data/local state only, no real financial transactions — but with clear interfaces/services so a licensed payment provider can be integrated later.

## 54. Legal / Compliance Awareness

Because GER NAADGAI is a children's product with physical sales, digital content, QR codes, and future marketplace/wallet functionality, design with: parental consent, age-appropriate experience, privacy protection, secure authentication, purchase approval, transaction records, refund support, child safety, no gambling mechanics, no direct child-to-child financial transactions. **Real-money wallet/payment functionality must remain disabled in the MVP** until the appropriate Mongolian legal/regulatory structure is confirmed.

## 55. Product Principle

GER NAADGAI is **not** "buy a toy and play an app." It is: *"Every physical collectible is a key to a larger Mongolian world."* The physical product creates discovery. The QR creates connection. The digital world creates engagement. The collection creates retention. The stories create cultural value. The games create fun. The annual zodiac cycle creates long-term continuity. The marketplace creates repeat purchase opportunities. The 12-year journey creates the long-term IP.

## 56. Premium Mongolian Illustration World

Illustration quality is a **top priority**, built around exceptionally beautiful, original Mongolian illustrations. Do not use generic stock illustrations, generic fantasy landscapes, generic Asian aesthetics, or cheap children's cartoon graphics. Should feel like a premium contemporary Mongolian children's picture book combined with a collectible game world, communicating: *"Mongolia is beautiful, magical, vast and worth discovering."*

## 57. Mongolian Landscape System

A modular illustration library of Mongolia's landscapes: endless steppe, rolling grasslands, Gobi desert, Altai mountains, Khangai mountains, snow-covered peaks, rocky valleys, alpine lakes, rivers, sand dunes, forested mountains, open plains, sunrise/sunset over the steppe, star-filled night sky, moonlit landscape, and all four seasons. Must be recognizable as Mongolia, with strong composition and atmosphere — every landscape a place the child wants to explore.

## 58. Mongolian Nature & Animals

Beautiful illustrated representations of: Mongolian horse, Przewalski's horse, Bactrian camel, Mongolian gazelle, snow leopard, argali, ibex, wolf, fox, eagle, saker falcon, marmot, swan, crane, sheep, goat, cattle — culturally and anatomically recognizable. Not every animal needs to become a generic cute mascot; some can be expressive characters, others elegant natural-history illustrations.

## 59. Cultural Illustration System

Illustrate: Mongolian ger, toono, traditional saddle, morin khuur, deel, boots, horse tack, milk container, traditional tools, shagai, bow and arrows, wrestling, Naadam, horse racing, archery, Mongolian family, children playing traditional games, nomadic daily life, moving camp, cooking, milking animals, riding horses — simplified enough for the game, but culturally accurate.

## 60. Illustration Style

A distinctive GER NAADGAI illustration language combining: contemporary editorial illustration, Mongolian folk-art influence, screen-print/risograph texture, subtle paper grain, hand-drawn linework, sophisticated flat shapes, controlled texture, atmospheric landscapes, warm natural colors. Avoid excessive gradients, 3D-rendered Pixar-like aesthetics, generic anime, overly childish clip-art. A child should want to save the artwork as a poster; a parent should feel comfortable displaying it at home; a tourist should want to buy the physical product because the artwork represents Mongolia beautifully.

## 61. Visual Storytelling

Illustrations should not just decorate the UI — they should tell stories. E.g. instead of a generic "Horse screen," show a Mongolian horse running across an enormous steppe at sunrise. The environment should be as important as the character.

## 62. Environmental Collections

Collectible illustration environments (e.g. Summer Steppe, Gobi Journey, Altai Mountains, Khangai Forest, Winter Mongolia, Night Under the Steppe Sky, Naadam Day, Tsagaan Sar, Nomadic Camp), unlockable as the user progresses, reusable as digital ger backgrounds, profile backgrounds, story scenes, game backgrounds, loading screens, collection cards, posters, and social media artwork.

## 63. Digital Ger World

The digital ger exists inside a beautiful illustrated Mongolian environment that changes as the user collects — e.g. simple ger + open steppe at start; more animals appear after 3 collectibles; a richer nomadic camp after 5; new landscape elements after story unlocks; special seasonal/zodiac environments after special editions. The world should visually reflect progress.

## 64. Seasonal Art Direction

Four seasonal visual worlds: **Spring** (soft green steppe, newborn animals), **Summer** (bright green grassland, blue sky), **Autumn** (golden steppe, warm orange tones, long shadows), **Winter** (snow-covered ger camp, deep blue sky, warm interior light) — letting the app evolve naturally throughout the year.

## 65. Tsagaan Sar Visual World

The 2027 launch should have a special Tsagaan Sar environment: winter Mongolian landscape, snow, warm ger, traditional clothing, family gathering, milk tea, buuz, aaruul, traditional decorations, zodiac symbolism, soft moonlight, warm interior glow — warm, elegant, authentic, and clearly Mongolian (not a generic Lunar New Year theme).

## 66. Illustration as a Collectible

Illustrations themselves can become collectible content (e.g. "COLLECTIBLE ART #01 — THE FIRST STEPPE," "#02 — ALTAI MORNING," "#03 — GOBI JOURNEY"), unlocked on completing certain collections and displayable inside the user's digital ger.

## 67. Art Direction Quality Bar

Every major illustration should pass: (1) Does it immediately feel Mongolian? (2) Is the landscape beautiful enough to be standalone artwork? (3) Strong composition? (4) Does it tell a story? (5) Works at both mobile-screen and poster scale? (6) Feels premium? (7) Fits the GER NAADGAI identity? (8) Would a child want to explore it? (9) Would a parent appreciate it? (10) Would a tourist want to take it home? If no — improve it.

## 68. Art Asset System

Reusable layers/components: Background, Midground, Character, Objects, Clouds, Mountains, Vegetation, Light, Ornament, Texture — reusable across app, website, packaging, collectible cards, posters, social media, retail displays, animation, and games.

## 69. Art Direction Reference

*"Contemporary Mongolian children's picture book meets premium collectible game."* Hand-crafted, culturally rooted illustration as the emotional layer; a modern, clean UI as the functional layer. UI elements must never cover or destroy the artwork.

## 70. Originality

All illustrations must be original GER NAADGAI artwork — no imitating or copying existing Mongolian brands, artists, posters, museum graphics, or commercial illustrations. Use Mongolian cultural references respectfully and accurately. Goal: a new, recognizable **"GER NAADGAI STYLE."**

## 71. Language System — Mongolian First

GER NAADGAI is a Mongolian brand. The **primary language of the entire digital experience is Mongolian (Монгол хэл)**; English is secondary/supporting. Default app language: Mongolian. Every major UI element, story, instruction, button, game instruction, collection name, character description and notification must have a Mongolian version first, with English as a smaller secondary translation where appropriate.

## 72. Language Hierarchy

**Primary:** Mongolian Cyrillic. **Secondary:** English. Mongolian text is always visually dominant; English is smaller and visually secondary. Example: "ШИНЭ НЭЭЛТ / NEW DISCOVERY," "ЦУГЛУУЛГАДАА НЭМЭХ / ADD TO COLLECTION."

## 73. Child-First Mongolian UX

Mongolian must feel natural, warm and child-friendly — never overly formal, bureaucratic, or textbook-style (not government-document / museum-description / school-textbook / legal-document language). E.g. prefer "Бүгдийг нь цуглуулаарай!" over "Цуглуулгын бүрдэлтийг гүйцээнэ үү." Prefer "Шинэ найзаа нээ!" over "Дижитал дүрийг нээх." Prefer "Тоглоцгооё!" over "Тоглоомыг эхлүүлэх."

## 74. Brand Voice

Warm, curious, playful, imaginative, encouraging, culturally proud — never cheaply childish. GER NAADGAI speaks like a friendly guide inviting a child to discover Mongolia (e.g. "Монголын нэг жижигхэн хэсгийг нээ." / "Чиний дараагийн нээлт юу бол?" / "Шинэ адал явдал эхэллээ!").

## 75. Mongolian Character Naming

Mongolian names are primary (АДУУ/HORSE, ТЭМЭЭ/CAMEL, ХОНЬ/SHEEP, ЯМАА/GOAT, ҮХЭР/COW) — English names must never be visually dominant. Cultural characters/stories always use the original Mongolian name first (ЭРХИЙ МЭРГЭН / Erkhii Mergen).

## 76. Mongolian Script

Traditional Mongolian script (e.g. ᠭᠡᠷ ᠨᠠᠭᠠᠳᠠᠭᠠᠢ) may be used as a decorative cultural layer — logo, decorative headings, collectible cards, packaging, special cultural moments, badges, ornaments — but must **not** replace Cyrillic for important UX instructions; Cyrillic remains the primary readable language.

## 77. English Usage

English supports international audiences, tourists, and future global expansion: secondary translations, international website, tourist-facing retail, product packaging where useful, investor materials, international marketing. The English translation should never make the product feel English-first — GER NAADGAI remains fundamentally Mongolian.

## 78. Language Toggle

A language setting — **ХЭЛ / LANGUAGE** — 🇲🇳 Монгол / 🇬🇧 English, defaulting to Монгол. Switching to English changes the entire UI; preference stored locally.

## 79. Bilingual Collectible Cards

Physical collectible cards: Mongolian first, larger typography; English smaller, e.g. "ЯМАА / GOAT," "ТАВАН ХОШУУ МАЛ / FIVE TREASURES OF MONGOLIA," with a short Mongolian cultural sentence and its English translation.

## 80. Story Language

Stories written primarily in natural Mongolian (e.g. "Эрт урьд цагт, Монголын уудам талд..."), reading like a real Mongolian children's storybook. Do not translate word-for-word if it sounds unnatural — preserve emotional and cultural meaning over literal translation.

## 81. Game Instructions

Extremely simple Mongolian (e.g. "Морио давхиул!" / RIDE!, "Саадыг дав!" / JUMP!, "Оноо цуглуулаарай!" / COLLECT POINTS!). Avoid long instructions; prefer visual instructions wherever possible.

## 82. Cultural Accuracy

Preserve Mongolian terminology for cultural concepts (Гэр, Тооно, Унь, Хана, Шагай, Наадам, Цагаан сар, Дээл, Морин хуур, Эрхий мэргэн, Таван хошуу мал) rather than unnecessarily replacing them with generic English equivalents; English may supply a supporting explanation.

## 83. International Expansion

Architecture should support future locales beyond `mn` (Mongolian, default) and `en` (English) — e.g. `ja`, `ko`, `zh` — but **do not build additional languages now**. Focus on excellent Mongolian first.

## 84. Final Language Principle

GER NAADGAI should feel like *"a Mongolian child's own Mongolian world, proudly carrying its national heritage,"* not *"an international children's game translated into Mongolian."* Mongolian is the original language; English is the translation. Cultural identity, storytelling, humor, terminology and emotional tone must originate from Mongolian.

## 85. Social Sharing — Discovery Moments

Allow users to celebrate and share important discovery moments (new collectible unlocked, collection completed, special story unlocked, mini-game won, limited-edition character discovered) via an optional "SHARE" action. Goal: turn exciting GER NAADGAI discoveries into beautiful shareable cultural artwork — **not** to encourage children to publicly expose personal information.

## 86. Shareable Discovery Card

On unlocking a new character, offer a "SHARE" action that generates a beautiful, automatically designed share card — looking like a premium GER NAADGAI collectible poster, not a screenshot of the app.

## 87. Social Formats

Generate optimized, auto-adapting formats: Instagram Story (1080×1920), Instagram Post (1080×1350), Square (1080×1080), Facebook Story (1080×1920), Facebook Post (adaptable 4:5/1:1).

## 88. Share Card Design

Every share card includes: GER NAADGAI logo, character illustration, character name in Mongolian (with smaller English translation), collection name, collectible number, a short cultural phrase, and an optional status ("NEW DISCOVERY" / "COLLECTION COMPLETE" / "2027 SPECIAL EDITION" / "12-YEAR JOURNEY"), on a beautiful Mongolian landscape background — strong enough to work as an independent poster.

## 89. Discovery Share Examples

See spec examples: "NEW DISCOVERY 🐐 — ЯМАА / GOAT — ТАВАН ХОШУУ МАЛ #04"; "COLLECTION COMPLETE! — ТАВАН ХОШУУ МАЛ 5/5 — Бүгдийг нь цуглууллаа!"; "✨ SPECIAL DISCOVERY — 2027 ХОНЬ ЖИЛ — LIMITED EDITION"; "📖 STORY UNLOCKED — ЭРХИЙ МЭРГЭН."

## 90. Share Flow

**NEW DISCOVERY → character reveal animation → [ADD TO COLLECTION] → [SHARE YOUR DISCOVERY]**, offering Instagram Story / Instagram Post / Facebook Story / Facebook Post / Save Image. Use native mobile/web sharing where available; otherwise generate and download/save the share image.

## 91. Child Safety (Sharing)

Share cards must **never** automatically include: child's real name, age, location, school, personal information, account ID, wallet balance, or purchase history. No public profiles, no direct messaging, no way for sharing to let children contact strangers. The share card focuses entirely on the collectible and artwork.

## 92. Parent Approval (Sharing)

Social sharing is controlled through parent settings: **ALLOW SHARING** or **DISABLE SHARING**. For younger users, sharing should require parent approval where appropriate. The prototype may simulate this setting.

## 93. Viral Cultural Content

The share system should turn discoveries into interesting cultural content even for people unfamiliar with the app — e.g. not "I got an eagle!" but "БҮРГЭД / EAGLE — MONGOLIAN NATURE COLLECTION" over a dramatic Mongolian mountain landscape.

## 94. Collection Share

Completing a collection generates a special premium poster (e.g. "ТАВАН ХОШУУ МАЛ 🐎🐫🐑🐐🐄 — 5/5 COMPLETE — Монголын таван эрдэнийг цуглууллаа.") with all five characters together against a Mongolian landscape.

## 95. 12-Year Journey Share

A special shareable card for 12-Year Journey progress (e.g. "1/12 YEARS," building up to "12/12 — 12 YEARS COMPLETE — Монголын 12 жилийн аяллыг цуглууллаа.") — intended to become a very special collectible achievement.

## 96. Social Design Principle

Sharing should never feel like advertising — it should feel like *"I discovered something beautiful."* Shared artwork must be beautiful, cultural, collectible, premium, instantly understandable, and visually recognizable as GER NAADGAI, so that someone unfamiliar with the brand still thinks *"What is this beautiful Mongolian character?"*

## 97. Share Template System

Reusable share templates, all on the same visual identity: New Discovery, Rare Discovery, Special Edition, Collection Complete, Story Unlocked, Mini-Game Achievement, 12-Year Journey, Seasonal Discovery, Cultural Fact, GER NAADGAI Anniversary.

## 98. Social Content Loop

**CHILD BUYS GER → OPENS GER → DISCOVERS CHARACTER → SCANS QR → UNLOCKS DIGITAL CHARACTER → CREATES BEAUTIFUL SHARE CARD → SHARES TO INSTAGRAM/FACEBOOK → FRIENDS/FAMILY DISCOVER GER NAADGAI → NEW CUSTOMER DISCOVERS THE BRAND.** Sharing should support organic brand discovery without relying entirely on paid advertising.

## 99. QR → Instant Collectible Card → Share

The QR scan is one of the most important moments in the product. A successful scan must not simply redirect to a character page — it is a "magical discovery moment": **SCAN QR → SCANNING ANIMATION → GER NAADGAI REVEAL → NEW DISCOVERY → COLLECTIBLE CARD APPEARS → ADD TO MY COLLECTION → SHARE MY DISCOVERY.**

## 100. Collectible Card Reveal

Immediately after a successful scan, generate a beautiful digital collectible card — a premium digital version of the physical card — containing the GER NAADGAI wordmark, illustration, Mongolian + English name, collection name, number, and a "✦ DISCOVERED ✦" stamp once added. It should appear with a reveal animation, not a flat redirect.

## 101. Card Reveal Animation

**Screen darkens slightly → soft ambient cue → light appears → card rotates/flips into view → illustration reveals → name appears → collection name appears → "ШИНЭ НЭЭЛТ!"** Should feel magical and premium — explicitly **not** flashy like a casino or loot box. This is a cultural discovery, not gambling.

## 102. Card Content

Each digital card: GER NAADGAI logo, character illustration, Mongolian name (primary) + English name (secondary), collection, collectible number, rarity if applicable, a short cultural fact, discovery status, year/edition if applicable.

## 103. Share Immediately

After the card is revealed, **SHARE is a primary action alongside ADD** — **[ЦУГЛУУЛГАДАА НЭМЭХ]** and **[ХУВААЛЦАХ]** together. The user must be able to share the newly discovered card immediately, without navigating to another page first.

## 104. Shareable Card

Tapping ХУВААЛЦАХ/SHARE generates a separate, clean GER NAADGAI-branded social artwork — **never** a screenshot of the app UI. Beautiful enough to function as a standalone poster: large Mongolian landscape illustration, name, collection, number, a short cultural phrase, and the GER NAADGAI / MONGOLIA IN YOUR HANDS wordmark.

## 105. Share Formats

Instagram Story (1080×1920), Instagram Post (1080×1350), Square (1080×1080), Facebook Story (1080×1920), Facebook Post (1080×1350) — responsive templates.

## 106. Discovery Card Types

New Discovery (new character scanned), Special Discovery (limited-edition character), Collection Complete (all characters in a collection unlocked), Story Unlocked, Game Unlocked, 12-Year Milestone (progress in the 12-year journey).

## 107. Physical Card ↔ Digital Card

The physical collectible's card and the digital card should visually match (same name, number, GER NAADGAI branding), so the child feels: *"I own this character in real life AND in my GER NAADGAI world."*

## 108. Collection Card Gallery

After sharing, the card is automatically saved to МИНИЙ ЦУГЛУУЛГА / My Collection, using the same card design language: locked cards show silhouette + "???", unlocked cards show full artwork + number + name. The visual progression should make an incomplete collection feel exciting, not frustrating.

## 109. Share Privacy

The shared image must never include: child's real name, account ID, wallet balance, location, school, age, or other personal information. It contains only GER NAADGAI branding, the collectible, illustration, collection, and cultural information. Social sharing is always optional.

## 110. Core Experience Principle

The QR code is not a link — it is a **magic key**: **Physical GER → QR → DISCOVERY → DIGITAL CARD → COLLECTION → STORY → GAME → SHARE → NEXT DISCOVERY.** Every scan should feel like opening a tiny door into Mongolia.

## 111. Final QR Experience

*"I opened a tiny Mongolian ger. I found something inside. I scanned it. I discovered a new character. I got a beautiful card. I added it to my collection. I can play with it. I can share what I discovered. What will I find in the next ger?"*
